National Academic & Research Awards 2026 – 2027

Recognizing Excellence Across Every Stage of the Academic Journey

IFRE presents prestigious awards in the Essence of Excellence. Click a category below to explore awards, then click any award to view full eligibility, selection criteria, and required documents. Selection is purely merit-based.

Academic Excellence Award (M.Tech)

For Outstanding Academic Performance
+

Recognizes the M.Tech student who demonstrates exceptional performance in the prescribed academic curriculum.

Selection may consider

  • Highest CGPA / academic score
  • Consistent semester performance
  • Performance across core and elective subjects
  • Academic standing within the class / cohort
  • Overall performance in the prescribed curriculum

Supporting Documents

  • Official mark sheets / grade cards
  • CGPA or percentage record
  • Bonafide / student status certificate
  • Other academic records requested by IFRE

Important Notes for All Applicants

  • Submit your updated CV along with a passport-size photograph.
  • Application submission online is free and open to all academic field experts and students across Arts, Science, Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Nursing, and other disciplines.
  • The selection committee evaluates applications based on academic performance, teaching experience, research achievements, publications, and books.
  • Selected nominees pay a non-refundable registration fee.
  • Every award includes a framed certificate with a unique verification number, a memento, and courier delivery.
  • Certificates can be verified online through the Verify Certificate page.
  • Suitable for Co-Curricular Activity (CCA) / Academic Recognition requirements at the State or National level.
  • IFRE may request additional documentation for verification purposes where necessary.
