Registration Fee & Cancellation Policy

Registration Fee

Registration fee is mandatory after selecting the concerned award. The fee is non-refundable.

What You Receive

Soft and hard copy of the framed award certificate, along with a standard quality memento / plaque.

Delivery

The hard copy of the framed certificate and memento will be sent by courier to your postal address.

📢 Calling for Awards

Applications are open now. IFRE welcomes submissions from students, faculty, researchers, and academic leaders across India. Apply at your earliest convenience.

Please Note

  • Application submission online is free and open to all students, faculty, researchers, and academic professionals.
  • Selection is purely merit-based — based on academic performance, teaching experience, and research achievements.
  • The registration fee is mandatory after selection and is non-refundable.
  • Award kit will be dispatched via courier or post.
  • Certificates can be verified online through the Verify Certificate page.
